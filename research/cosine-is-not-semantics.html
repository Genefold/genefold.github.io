Cosine Is Not Semantics: Learning a Spectral Metric from the Embedding Corpus as alternative approach

About the paper

The paper defines a normalized spectral kernel from a corpus-derived feature graph, examines its mathematical properties, and sets out controls for evaluating whether it improves semantic alignment.
