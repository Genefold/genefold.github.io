Graph Wiring: Accessing more information via Feature-Space. A Constructive Separation and Conditional Coding Analysis

A preprint analysing how feature-space graph structure can reveal relationships that item-vector geometry alone does not determine.

About the paper

The paper constructs corpora with identical row Gram matrices but different feature graphs, then analyses how graph-based relevance can distinguish cases that row geometry cannot.
